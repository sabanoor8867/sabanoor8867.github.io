About

I am a PhD researcher at Ghent University, in the Department of Animal Sciences and Aquatic Ecology and the Bovi-Analytics lab. In 2027 I will do a research internship at the Bovi-Analytics Lab of Cornell University, with a grant of the Research Foundation Flanders (FWO). My promoters are Miel Hostens (Cornell University) and Jeroen Degroote (Ghent University). My thesis is titled "Ontology-driven animal health: a prerequisite towards federated learning".

From 2023 to 2026 I was co-lead of the data work package of the EU Horizon 2020 project DECIDE, where diagnostic data on cattle, pigs, poultry and salmon came from laboratories in several countries. I designed the Livestock Health Ontology to give these data a shared structure and meaning, compared ways of giving access to them, and built a federated learning system in which the data stay with each laboratory. Before my PhD I taught computer science at two universities in Pakistan.

Research

Ontology

Livestock Health Ontology (LHO)

An ontology for animal health surveillance data of cattle, pigs, poultry and salmon. Laboratories map their own columns and codes to it once, and the same query then works on the data of every laboratory. Published on AgroPortal, BioPortal and GitHub.

Documentation and queries AgroPortal Conference paper (2024) Systematic review (2026)
Data access

Direct, centralized or federated access

A comparison of three ways of giving access to the same animal health data, including decentralized access over Solid Pods, with query performance, a FAIR assessment and practical guidelines for choosing between them.

Paper Code
Federated learning

SolidFL

Federated learning over Solid Pods. A model is trained next to each laboratory's Pod and only model updates are shared. The ontology gives all laboratories the same features. Evaluated on bovine respiratory disease data from six laboratories in four countries. Manuscript in preparation.

Demo video Code (public soon)
LLM application

LHO knowledge-graph assistant

An assistant that answers questions about livestock health data through an MCP server on a knowledge graph, with an open-source model that runs locally. It answered 39 of 40 test questions correctly, and every number in an answer is checked against the data. The demo uses synthetic data.
